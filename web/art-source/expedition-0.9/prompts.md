# Sprinter animation derivative: built-in image generation

Tool: built-in `image_gen`, edit mode, transparent background. No API/CLI fallback or third-party asset source. Edit target: the retained [Sprinter source](../expedition-0.3/sprinter-original.png). Selected raw output: [sprinter-parts-generated.png](sprinter-parts-generated.png). The generated image is 1254×1254 RGBA despite the requested dimensions/layout. It contains nine disconnected large parts; extraction follows the actual alpha components rather than assuming the requested grid was honored.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: production animation-ready separated parts sheet for ONE existing game character, Sprinter. The input is the authoritative edit target: a LEFT-facing coral-orange and slate-teal digitigrade runner with amber eye, swept crest, spotted hide, short tucked forearms, long two running legs and tapered forked tail. Preserve this exact character's anatomy, silhouette, facial details, colors, painterly shading, outline weight, perspective and upper-left lighting. Do NOT invent a different creature or redraw its style. Primary request: derive nine isolated anatomical cutout parts from the same illustration for a controlled jointed animation rig. One 1536x1536 transparent square sheet, strict equal 3x3 grid, one part in each cell in this exact reading order: (1) COMPLETE HEAD+NECK+TORSO+HIPS+TAIL as one continuous body, with BOTH legs and BOTH small forearms removed cleanly, plausibly filling only the small occluded body areas; (2) NEAR coral upper hind leg from hip to knee; (3) NEAR teal lower hind leg from knee to ankle; (4) NEAR clawed three-toed foot; (5) FAR shadow-teal upper hind leg hip to knee; (6) FAR shadow-teal lower hind leg knee to ankle; (7) FAR three-toed foot; (8) NEAR entire small coral/teal forearm and tucked clawed hand; (9) FAR entire small shadow-teal forearm and hand. All nine parts must be usable separate opaque figure textures on actual transparent alpha, with generous transparent gaps and no overlap between cells. Show rounded, slightly extended attachment caps at cut joints so parts can overlap without gaps when rotated. Each part is a direct faithful derivative of the source material, not a new design. Body remains LEFT-facing and takes most of its cell width; isolate each smaller part clearly within its own cell, at high resolution suitable for proportional rescaling during atlas packing. Maintain consistent contour and materials across parts; no residual legs or arms baked into the body layer, no extra limbs, no ground, no shadows outside the actual parts, no colored background, no glow, no haze, no checkerboard, no labels, no letters, no numbers, no guides or grid lines, no watermark. The purpose is to move the existing limbs independently while keeping the same torso/face pixels stable across every animation pose.
```

## Conversion and review

`python3 scripts/build-sprinter-animation.py` uses the existing Pillow installation. It identifies alpha≥32 connected components with >500 pixels, preserves original alpha in a four-pixel halo, and excludes disconnected noise. This is extraction of already-separated parts, followed by affine articulation and atlas packing; no programmatic repainting occurs. [conversion.json](conversion.json) records component bounds, sequence ranges, production geometry, byte count and SHA-256. [sources.json](sources.json) records the retained reference, static fallback, raw generated source, baked PNG, production WebP and conversion script hashes.

The production sprite atlas is 1280×720, 48 cells of 160×120, displayed at 128×96. Fixed foot baseline: y=105 source / y=84 world. WebP quality85/method6. The 118,434-byte atlas is the only new production raster. Cropped parts, raw generated PNG and lossless baked PNG stay outside `public/`.

Review rejected the first small-cap assembly: its hip attachment disc remained exposed and its forearms were too large. The retained script places the thigh pivot inside the painted muscle, overlaps caps with the hip and reduces forearm scale. A 256×192-frame bake cost 254,066 bytes; the selected 160×120 bake reduces that by 135,632 bytes. Detail beyond the actual battlefield scale is visibly softer and is not presented as a high-resolution character sheet.

The source derivative is not pixel-identical to the static illustration: the hips are compact and the tail tip differs slightly. It retains the coral/teal materials, amber eye, swept crest, spots, left-facing biped anatomy and silhouette. Torso/face shading does not get regenerated between poses. Affine resampling and lossy atlas encoding can change edge pixels. Further art direction and genuine human preference testing remain open; this is one pilot, not authorization to replace the roster.
