# Original flagship identity review

Reviewed 2026-10-31 against the Expedition 0.4 starting commit `96f8297cc88be416d4e5d49270b7f623f44881b3`. This is a bounded design and provenance review, not a legal clearance opinion or a search of trademark registrations.

## Decision

Use **Frontier Flagship** as the public-facing defender name, paired with an independently designed expedition gunship. Retain the internal `falcon` kind, existing combat/economy values, original sprite references, school-project history, and original-art comparison. Renaming and drawing an original silhouette address a visible franchise dependency without rewriting game rules.

The official [Star Wars Databank entry](https://www.starwars.com/databank/millennium-falcon) identifies Millennium Falcon as a specific Star Wars spacecraft. Its name and familiar silhouette therefore convey an existing franchise identity rather than this game's own expedition universe. This is a design inference from that official entry, not a determination of the legal status of the repository's particular school asset.

The [U.S. Copyright Office's derivative-work guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/eco/help-limitation.html) explains that adaptations can contain preexisting material and that creating a new version is an exclusive right of the copyright owner. The [Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html) explains that fair use depends on the circumstances; there is no fixed percentage-change rule. A new coat of paint or attribution should not be treated as permission to reproduce protected franchise expression.

The [USPTO's likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) considers sound, appearance, meaning, commercial impression, and related goods/services. Its [search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching) describes comprehensive clearance as broader than one exact-name database search. “Frontier Flagship” is a descriptive development choice, not a claim of availability, registration, exclusivity, or completed clearance.

## Art brief and acceptance checks

- Original elongated, compact hull with a central raised bridge, three readable weapon pods, broad cream/mint armor, navy undercarriage, and limited amber/coral accents. Shared upper-left lighting and painted shapes tie it to the existing crew.
- A squat, grounded or very-low-hover stance makes a durable lane defender rather than implying free flight. A right-facing tapered nose makes fire direction legible. Keep it attributable to one grid cell, with room for health indicators and neighboring defenders.
- Use visibly different plasma, precision, and demolition hardware to communicate the preserved three-ammunition volley. Cosmetic pods do not change damage, targeting, range, collision, or ammunition rules.
- Avoid the recognizable circular saucer with split forward mandibles, offset cockpit corridor, dorsal radar dish, franchise markings, or recreations of the school image. Do not supply franchise images as generation references. The negative design list is a conservative creative choice, not a legal checklist guaranteeing independence.
- Review both the portrait and dense battlefield at normal play scale on Canyon, Bridge, and Nest. Reject an output that still reads as the Star Wars ship, becomes a large floating disc, obscures cells, or has inconsistent weapon perspective. Preserve exact prompts, untouched source, conversion details, and hashes.

## Historical and publication boundaries

Keep the original name in historical documentation and source manifests where it identifies the actual school asset. Preserve the original team credits. The comparison switch may show retained school art for this local development milestone; it does not establish rights to distribute that art publicly. The existing [asset-provenance limits](../../../ASSET_CREDITS.md) remain relevant to **all** retained school assets, including those still reachable through comparison or destination previews.

Before a future public launch, resolve the specific retained assets' provenance/terms or remove/replace their public delivery. This milestone does not claim to clear every character name, historical asset, or future brand, and does not publish the game. No third-party contact was made.
