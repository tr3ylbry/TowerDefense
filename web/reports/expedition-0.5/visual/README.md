The final mixed-map tests use two Bushes, six Astros, six Zeus, one TARS, Tree, Flagship, Explosive, Startrell and Asteroid: twenty actual purchases/1,865 spending. Every type is present before the screenshot; ordinary pause/style comparison preserves all 54 cell labels and credits. Every map then wins and its real income/spending ledger reconciles. This is a reproducible mixed formation rather than a claim that a novice will discover it or that every possible dense arrangement is readable.

Root visual review accepted the four selected new defenders and the new Bridge/Nest/acid treatment after checking complete feet/equipment, facing, cell boundaries, role silhouettes, neighboring units, health indicators, enemies and projectile contrast. Tree is taller/narrower than Bush; orange demolition hardware differs from cyan plasma; Startrell remains light; compact Flagship has three readable weapons without overlapping another cell. The static acid X is color-independent. Bridge's background zoom keeps its illustrated rails at the perimeter; logical grid/acid geometry is unchanged.

The benchmark's [normal/constrained production captures](../performance/README.md) are separate specialist formations and supplement the complete-roster browser review. Browser resizing/CSS zoom do not validate real phones, native Safari, OS text settings, screen readers or physical GPU behavior. All 8 generated-source records and the rejected wide version are preserved in [provenance](../../../public/art/expedition/README-0.5.md).
