## Crew-card normalization

[Desktop Expedition strip](crew-cards-desktop.png) and [original-art strip](crew-cards-desktop-original.png) are actual Chromium test captures; [phone Explosive](crew-cards-phone.png) and [phone Falcon](crew-cards-phone-falcon.png) are manual in-app-browser views. The explicit top row separates portrait and price; full names wrap in the aligned lower area. Current labels retain two lines of space, while longer future names can expand the row. No price/name overlap, clipping or shortened labels is needed. Both art styles, all nine units and three widths have geometry regressions, alongside the retained touch/scroll/focus tests.

## Mission economy terminal

The user's victory screenshot identified the receipt's centered prose as difficult to scan. [Desktop ledger](mission-ledger-desktop.png) and [phone ledger scrolled to its ending balance](mission-ledger-phone.png) are actual Chromium regression captures after an ordinary one-defender loss. [Preview context](mission-ledger-preview.png) shows the same actual outcome in the in-app browser. This intentionally short UI check is not a balance test or independent participant session. All values come from the final mission snapshot: 230 starting credits + 240 supply + 25 bounties − 50 purchases = 445 ending credits. The dark mint panel, static fine lines, three readouts, aligned signed entries and prominent balance use existing CSS/fonts. The expanded overlay scrolls on short fields; keyboard toggle, phone/landscape geometry, ending-balance access and Play again are covered without changing the original receipt scenario's assertions.
