## Generation terms evidence

OpenAI's [individual Terms of Use, effective January 1, 2026](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/), assign OpenAI's rights in output to the user, to the extent allowed by law. They also state that output may be nonunique, do not extend that assignment to third-party output, and place input-rights/content responsibilities on the user. The [business Services Agreement](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/) has a corresponding conditional output-ownership provision and separate scope. These primary sources support documenting a service-terms basis for generated output; they do not establish third-party design clearance or the original team's consent. **Owner confirmation needed:** which account/agreement applied to the recorded generations, acceptance of included designs, and any separate contributor rights. No account type, contract acceptance or legal approval was inferred from the source images.

## Identity findings

**TARS is the concrete remaining visible identity concern.** It is a playable label and appears in armor explanations, briefings and descriptions. Paramount's [official Interstellar home-media announcement](https://ir.paramount.com/news-releases/news-release-details/director-christopher-nolans-awe-inspiring-masterpiece/) expressly identifies TARS and CASE as film characters. The retained name therefore has an evidenced franchise association rather than a speculative ordinary-word objection. The 0.2 prompt deliberately rejects the film's four-block body and creates a single cabin torso, two short legs, sensor and asymmetric tool. That distinction is useful provenance, not a legal conclusion. **Owner decision:** approve a distinct public-facing name and review its existing independent design, or obtain appropriate rights review. No automatic rename or combat/content rewrite was made.

**Millennium Falcon:** the official [Star Wars Databank](https://www.starwars.com/databank/Millennium-Falcon) identifies the franchise ship. Its school imagery is excluded. The playable visible unit remains the independently designed **Frontier Flagship**. The legacy internal `falcon`/`millennium-falcon` identifiers remain to preserve definitions/save compatibility; they do not load excluded assets. The credits retain a factual historical explanation and links to the preserved school source, not an endorsement or grant. **Owner decision:** review that historical wording/link as part of final publication copy and confirm the accepted Flagship design/terms. No claim that renaming alone clears a copied design.

**Other labels/concepts:** Astro Joe, Astro Joe X, Money Bush/Tree, Asteroid, Moon Zeus, Startrell Cluggins, Scout, Grunt, Sprinter, ManHunter, Tank and Gargantua were inspected in current content and recorded prompts. No specific franchise source/permission problem was established for these names by this inspection. Ordinary military/science-fiction words and the mythological Zeus reference are not automatically treated as prohibited. This was not an exhaustive trademark search, legal opinion or promise that no third-party claim exists. No wholesale character rename was justified.
